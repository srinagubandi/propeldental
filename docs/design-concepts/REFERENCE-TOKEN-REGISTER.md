# Propel Dental Design Concepts — Reference Token Register

**Purpose:** Three award-recognized adjacent references inform reusable design rules only. No reference layout, brand language, assets, client identities, or proprietary content may be copied.

| ID | Reference and recognition evidence | Observed reusable rule | Adaptation for Propel Dental | Restriction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | [WAX Healthcare Marketing Agency][1]. WAX’s Alera Health work received Gold for Website Design at the 2024 Healthcare Digital Marketing Awards.[2] | Start with a compact positioning statement, organize capabilities by decision outcome, and keep proof near the decision it supports. | Use a concise full-arch growth positioning line; group the offer into **strategy**, **demand**, **creative**, and **measurement**; add a clearly labeled proof system. | Do not copy wording, mascot, client logos, brand identifiers, or performance claims. |
| R-02 | [Superpower][3]. Awwwards lists Superpower as Site of the Day on August 13, 2024.[4] | Use a clear operating sequence, compact benefit modules, and deliberately stacked mobile content rather than a reduced desktop grid. | Present P90 as a visible three-part operating model. Recompose evidence, case studies, and contact at narrow widths as single-column decision panels. | Do not copy visual motifs, product assets, pricing, health claims, or custom motion. |
| R-03 | [Analogue Agency][5]. Awwwards lists it as Site of the Day on May 22, 2024.[6] | Let selected work carry proof. Use a restrained top-level navigation and an editorially paced sequence from positioning to work to people to contact. | Create a case-study-led proof layer with team context and a consultative `Get in touch` route. | Do not copy typography, marquee/scroll treatment, project imagery, client names, or agency language. |

## Adapted token sheet

| Token family | Concept-safe guidance | Why it fits the baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Logo treatment | Retain the supplied Propel Dental identity without alteration. Use clear space equal to the cap-height of the wordmark. | Meets the stated brand-retention constraint. |
| Accent | Keep orange as the decisive interactive and emphasis color; do not use it as body-text color on light surfaces without contrast validation. | Preserves the live brand’s high-contrast signal. |
| Type hierarchy | Use one dominant display role, one practical UI/sans role, and small uppercase labels only for navigation, taxonomy, and proof metadata. | Converts the current strong hero hierarchy into a reusable system. |
| Evidence labels | Reserve a visible `Illustrative content` state for all concept-phase case studies and metrics. Production labels should show evidence type, owner/source, review date, and limitation. | Keeps prototype content from implying verified client outcomes. |
| Images | Use real team portraits and practice/project media only after rights and approvals. Until then, use abstract editorial placeholders that cannot be mistaken for patient imagery. | Aligns with the healthcare/trust-sensitive boundary. |
| Motion | Make every visual complete as a static frame. Any later motion must be optional, respect reduced-motion preferences, and never contain essential meaning or the only contact path. | Avoids motion-led conversion failure on mobile. |
